{% elif "data" in page.slug or "engineering" in page.slug %}

What is MotherDuck and why do you use it instead of traditional data warehouses?

MotherDuck extends DuckDB into the cloud. It is a highly optimized columnar database designed for analytical queries that allows us to process millions of rows in milliseconds. We use it because it eliminates the massive infrastructure overhead, compute management, and excessive costs associated with platforms like Snowflake or Redshift for medium-sized data operations.

Can you connect data from tools like HubSpot, Salesforce, or custom proprietary APIs?

Yes. We specialize in building custom ETL/ELT pipelines and ingestion workers to pull data from any system that exposes a REST or GraphQL API, centralizing everything into your secure cloud data warehouse.

How do you guarantee the data in our live reporting dashboards is accurate?

We implement automated data quality and validation steps directly inside our transformation pipelines. If a source API sends corrupted, duplicated, or missing values, the pipeline isolates the bad records and alerts us immediately, preventing broken metrics from polluting your live production reports.

We have multiple web platforms running, but no clear view of our overall operations. Can you help us see the big picture?

This is where Data Engineering delivers the highest value. We connect your isolated websites, CRMs, and operational tools into a single warehouse. This allows you to track cause-and-effect across your entire business—such as how a specific digital platform update directly impacts customer retention or inventory levels.

Do you build the visual dashboards, or do you only handle the backend data pipelines?

We engineer the entire pipeline, from raw data extraction to the final visual layer. Once your data is centralized and cleaned in BigQuery or MotherDuck, we connect it to modern Business Intelligence tools (like Looker Studio or Power BI) to deliver clean, interactive, and automated dashboards for your executive team.

Our operational data contains sensitive customer and financial information. How do you ensure it remains secure during transit?

Security is built into every pipeline we design. We utilize enterprise-grade encryption (SSL/TLS) for data in transit, implement strict API authentication protocols, and apply Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) within the data warehouse to ensure sensitive information is only accessible to authorized team members.
